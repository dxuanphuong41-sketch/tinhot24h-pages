Creator-operated publishing workspace

tinhot24h helps review and upload short videos to TikTok drafts.

tinhot24h is the production workspace for the Vietnamese channel Lướt Mạng Có Chuyện. The creator generates a short video locally, reviews the narration, captions and metadata, then uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to upload the approved file as a draft.

End-to-end creator workflow

The application is operated by the channel owner, not by anonymous public users. It keeps publishing decisions under creator review.

Step 1Select source

The creator enters a link or topic for a short news-style video.

Step 2Render video

The local pipeline prepares narration, subtitles, visuals and metadata.

Step 3Review

The creator checks quality, source context, title, caption and hashtags.

Step 4Upload draft

The approved video is uploaded to TikTok as a draft for final posting in TikTok.

TikTok products and scopes

  • Login Kit lets the creator authorize their own TikTok account through OAuth.
  • user.info.basic is used only to confirm the authorized creator account.
  • Content Posting API sends the reviewed local video file to TikTok.
  • video.upload is used to upload creator-approved videos as drafts, not to publish public videos automatically.

Data and publishing controls

tinhot24h does not sell personal data, does not read private third-party content, and does not manage other users' accounts. The app stores OAuth tokens locally for the creator-operated workflow and uses them only for the authorized upload action.
