Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

tinhot24h is a private desktop application used by the operator of the channel “Lướt Mạng Có Chuyện” to create, review, and upload short-form videos. This policy explains what information is accessed during the TikTok authorization and upload workflow.

Information We Access

  • Basic TikTok account information needed to identify the authorized account, such as open id, display name, and avatar when provided by TikTok.
  • Permission to upload video files to the authorized TikTok account as drafts through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Local video files, thumbnails, scripts, captions, and metadata selected or generated by the operator.
  • Technical logs generated locally for troubleshooting rendering or upload errors.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate the account owner with TikTok.
  • To confirm that videos are uploaded only to the account authorized by the operator.
  • To upload creator-approved videos as TikTok drafts for final review inside TikTok.
  • To support the local workflow for rendering, subtitles, narration, branding, and metadata preparation.

Data Storage

OAuth tokens, generated videos, drafts, and logs are stored locally on the operator’s device. Content is sent to TikTok only when the operator starts an authorized upload action.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the selected platform APIs necessary to complete authentication or upload content requested by the operator.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local generated files and logs may be deleted by the operator from the device at any time. TikTok authorization can be revoked by disconnecting the app from the TikTok account or deleting the locally stored token file.

Children's Privacy

tinhot24h is not directed to children and is intended only for use by the channel operator.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the operator through the official “Lướt Mạng Có Chuyện” channel listed on the application website.
