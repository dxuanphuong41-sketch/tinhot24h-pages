Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

tinhot24h is a desktop application used to create, manage, and publish short-form videos for the channel “Lướt Mạng Có Chuyện”.

Information We Access

  • Basic TikTok account information required to identify the authorized user account.
  • Video publishing permissions granted by the user for content posted from the app.
  • Locally stored media and metadata selected or generated by the user.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate the account owner with TikTok.
  • To upload and publish user-generated videos to the authorized TikTok account.
  • To support the local workflow for video rendering, subtitles, narration, and publishing.

Data Storage

Tokens and generated files are stored locally on the user’s device unless the user explicitly uploads content to a supported platform.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the selected platform APIs necessary to complete authentication or publish content.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the app operator through the official channel associated with “Lướt Mạng Có Chuyện”.
